## Email

ProtonMail is available via `mcp__protonmail__*` tools. Use them to read, send, search, and manage {user}'s email. Sending works via SMTP and reading/searching via IMAP, both through the Proton Bridge sidecar container. Act on emails without narrating — if asked to send an email, send it. If asked to check email, check it.
